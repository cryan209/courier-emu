    Key Benefits of the Courier Model Over Sportster


 is the availability of additional ASL and HST protocols.
 ASL (Adaptive Speed Leveling) is a collective concept,
including the possibility of raising speed (fall-forward)
improving the quality of the line;
 possibility of asymmetrical connection on v.32(bis,Terbo)
protocols - i.e. the receiver and transmitter are analyzed
The optimal speed is set separately and on each channel,
which is very useful and gives a big win on anisotropic
lines;
 presence of Quick Retrain - while the retrains can pass for
very short time - up to 5 seconds, against 20-25 y regular
modems.
 HST (High Speed Technology) is an ultra-reliable protocol,
developed by the company USR specifically for work on bad lines.
Its distinctive feature is fantastic
stability (you can lift the tube on a parallel phone,
scream in line, have a parallel conversation on the phone - HST
lowers the speed to 4800, but does not fall and continues to work)
and very fast handshake, almost indistinguishable from 2400
handshake. These features create a special attraction
the HST protocol for working on Russian lines, famous for its
"quality." Many modem users forced by
bad line to work on 2400 due to the fact that on
The modems simply did not knit or constantly
cut off the connection, were just amazed when discovered that HST
successfully works on this line, without problems giving cps more
1000. It is for this reason that the world has such a high interest
to the HST protocol - it very successfully combines speed and
reliability.  But officially the HST protocol has only very
expensive series Courier, and in the cheap Sportster model of this
No protocol.

    Glossary.


* Courier HST DS (HST Dual Standard) - a modem that supports as
  standard protocols (ITU-T v.xx) and HST protocol.
* Fax (Facsimile) - a method of transferring a drawing or printed document by
  telephone line.
* HST (High Speed Technology) - new noise-resistant protocol,
  developed by USR and used in Courier models. Supports
  speeds 4800, 7200, 9600, 12000, 14400 and 16800 bps (judging by
  additional lines of the connect in
  the latest USR firmware plans to soon make HST 19200 and 21600).
* HST Cellular - protocol for cellular communication. Supports speeds 300,
  1200, 2400, 4800, 7200, 9600 and 12000 bps (in new models also 14400 and 16800
  bps).
* ASL (Adaptive Speed Leveling) - extension of the protocol v.42, developed
  company USR and used in Courier models. It is a set
  additional features such as Fall-Forward, Quick Retrain (tm), Assimetric
  transmission lineion, extra v.42 service frames etc.
* ITU-T (The Telecommunications Sector of the International Telecommunications
  Union) is an international organization that develops standards for
  telegraph, telephone and radio equipment. The old name is CCITT.
* NV RAM (NonVolatile Random Access Memory) - programmable memory, not
  reset when the power is switched off. Used to store the current
  configurations.
* Protocol - a system of rules and procedure, providing a connection between two
  or more devices.
* RAM (Random Access Memory) - programmable memory that does not persist at
  Off the power.
* ROM (Read Only Memory) - memory available for read only.
* US Robotics (USR) is an American company that produces Sportster series modems and
  Courier.
* v.32 - ITU-T standard for operation at 4800 and 9600 bps.
* v.32bis - ITU-T standard for operation at 4800, 7200, 9600, 12000 and
  14400 bps.
* v.32Terbo - AT&T standard for 4800, 7200, 9600, 12000,
  14400, 16800 and 19200 bps (with ASL up to 21600 bps).
* v.FC - Rockwell standard for operating at speeds 14400, 16800, 19200, 21600,
  24000, 26400 and 28800 bps.
* v.34 - ITU-T standard for operation at 2400, 4800, 7200, 9600, 12000,
  14400, 16800, 19200, 21600, 24000, 26400 and 28800 bps.
* v.42 - LAPM error correction, 1200 bps and above.
* v.42 bis - data compression, 1200 bps and above.
* MNP - error correction (levels 2, 3 and 4) and data compression (level 5), 1200 bps
  and higher.

Q: And what does DS mean in the name Courier HST DS?
A: This means Dual Standard - i.e. modem supports two standards: how
   ITU-T standards (v.xx - v.32bis, v.34 etc.) and its standard (HST).

Q: And why y me sometimes during the session light a light lamp AA on my
   Courier?
A: In the USR Courier modems, the AA light bulb during CONNECT on HST shows
   direction of high-speed channel HST. When the high-speed channel is directed to
   reception (16800/450) - AA does not burn when the high-speed channel is directed to
   parcel (450/16800) - AA lights up.

Q: Tell me the initialization string for my USR.
A: First you need to give the modem command AT&F1&W in the terminal. Then prescribe in
   software the first line ATZ, and the second AT&F1&K3. ATZ cleans the memory and it will save
   you're from all the glitches left of the previous connection. Example:
   Init1 AT Z|
   Init2 ~~AT &F1 &K3|

Q: And I initialize my modem with the ATZM0L0S0=0S1=0S6=255&F1 and no
   problems! Am I doing the right thing?
A: In virtually all modems (and USR is no exception) all that is introduced after ATZ
   - ignored. It also doesn’t make sense to put any commands before AT&F1,
   because this command loads the profile from the ROM and accordingly all the previous ones
   installations are rubbed.
